    How to practise

    1. Melody. Listen two or three times, then hum the tune on “mm” or “da-da”. Only the melody is scored. Move on when it reaches 80.
    2. Shadow slowly. The speaker plays at three-quarter speed and you speak at the same time. Wear headphones.
    3. Shadow. The same at normal speed.
    4. On your own. Say the phrase without the speaker. Then check the sounds in IPA and fix one thing at a time.

    While you record, your voice is drawn live as an orange line over the speaker’s blue melody. Try to keep the two lines together.
